The President Who Never Lived in the White House

About the channel

A museum you can watch in three minutes

What Past.Curious is

Past.Curious is the English-language history channel of Great Studios. Each episode is built like a museum exhibit: one object, one moment, one verified fact — presented up close, with the strange detail the textbooks leave out.

No myths invented, no history faked: when a beloved story turns out to be legend, the episode says so — and tells you what really happened instead.

Good questions

FAQ

What is Past.Curious?

Past.Curious is a museum-format history channel by Great Studios. Each episode takes one verified historical fact and follows it somewhere stranger — in about three minutes.

Where can I watch the episodes?

On YouTube, on the Past.Curious channel (@PastCuriousStories), and on the Past.Curious Facebook page, where episodes also appear as Reels and Stories.

Are the stories true?

Yes — episodes start from documented facts (dates, records, contemporary accounts). When a famous anecdote is actually a myth, the episode flags it as a myth and tells the verified version.

How often do new episodes come out?

Past.Curious is a new series: EP02 is the first published episode, EP01 is in production, and ten further episodes (EP03–EP12) are already mapped. Follow the channel on YouTube or Facebook to catch each release.

Where can I buy Past.Curious merch?

The shop is in preparation and will open from the Shop section of this page. Until then, the fastest way to hear about the first drop is to follow the channel.

Who makes Past.Curious?

Past.Curious is produced by Great Studios as part of its English-language history line, and shared through the Fundación Contacto Humano and Scope to the Future distribution circuit.
